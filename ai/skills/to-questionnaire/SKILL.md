# To Questionnaire

First establish the recipient, why their answers matter, what decision follows, and how much context they have. Do not ask them for facts already available in the repository or authoritative sources.

Group questions by decision theme. For each question:

- state why it matters;
- use the recipient's vocabulary;
- offer mutually exclusive options when useful;
- include a recommendation only when it will help rather than bias discovery;
- provide “unknown” or a bounded free-text response where reality may not fit the options.

Separate blocking questions from useful background. Avoid implementation jargon and compound questions.

Produce Markdown with context, response instructions, questions, and an optional notes section. Do not send, publish, or save it without the applicable approval.
