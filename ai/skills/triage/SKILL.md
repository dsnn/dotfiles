# Triage

Read the project's tracker instructions and label vocabulary. If none is configured, use neutral recommendations without creating a new taxonomy.

For each item determine:

- what outcome is requested and whether it is reproducible or verifiable;
- duplicates or overlapping work;
- impact, urgency, risk, and reversibility;
- missing information and who can provide it;
- dependencies and the smallest useful next action;
- recommended state: ready, needs information, blocked, duplicate, deferred, or declined.

Use `grilling` only when a selected item's decisions genuinely require the user. Keep facts and priority judgments separate.

Present proposed changes before mutating labels, assignments, issue bodies, or tracker state. Never close, delete, or reprioritize external work without explicit approval.
