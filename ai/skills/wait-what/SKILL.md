# Wait What

Start from the user's point of confusion, not from the beginning of the entire topic. State where the conversation is, why the point matters, and the one idea needed next.

Use short sentences, concrete nouns, and repository vocabulary. Expand acronyms and replace abstractions with a small example or analogy. Separate observed facts from recommendations.

Do not add new scope, repeat the same wording, or hide uncertainty behind simplification. End with one brief check for understanding or the next concrete choice.
